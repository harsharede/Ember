- Media controls on the notification page now work with more apps: the player is found through its notification too, and stays when an app leaves the title empty.
- A paused player's notification has its own play, pause and skip buttons.
- Wording: the notification light is called the light everywhere.
- While something plays, the Smart Stack goes to the player and stays there (swipe for other cards); it's added for as long as something plays even if the media card isn't chosen.
- Colours from wallpaper now really follow the wallpaper on every phone (Samsung kept them blue): Ember reads the wallpaper's colours itself.
